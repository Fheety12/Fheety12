## 👋 About Me

I am a passionate web developer based in Lagos, Nigeria, with a strong interest in building clean, responsive, and meaningful digital experiences. I enjoy solving real-world problems through technology and I believe great products should be both functional and delightful to use.

My journey in development began with the fundamentals of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and it has grown into creating modern interfaces and applications using React, Vite, and other frontend tools. I am constantly learning, refining my craft, and improving the way I build experiences for users.

I love combining creativity, problem-solving, and technology to deliver products that are practical, scalable, and visually engaging.
